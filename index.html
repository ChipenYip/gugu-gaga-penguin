What is the meme

Gugugaga Penguin is a viral AI-generated meme based on the Endministrator (Endmin) character from Arknights: Endfield by Hypergryph. The character's black-and-white outfit with yellow shoulder accents already resembled an emperor penguin, so fans started calling them "the penguin" — and someone took it the natural next step: chibi versions babbling baby-like "gugu gaga" sounds.

The first version appeared on Bilibili in February 2026 and spread to Douyin, TikTok, and X within two weeks. By March, there were Palworld mods, plushies, multiple landing pages chasing the search traffic, and at least one unresolved copyright dispute over the meme format.

Origin timeline

  • Feb 19, 2026 · Bilibili
    Creator 贱兄死顺盖 posts the first AI-generated chibi penguin Endmin clip with "gugu gaga" audio. The format is born.
  • Late Feb 2026
    Creator 涂山白白喵 produces the definitive AI-animated versions: drinking milk, cooking, chasing butterflies. These become the templates everyone iterates from.
  • Early March 2026
    Format jumps from Bilibili to Douyin, then to TikTok and X. English-language search traffic for "gugu gaga penguin" begins climbing.
  • Mid March 2026
    Original creator attempts to register copyright and demand a 10% royalty from merchandise. Community pushes back. The legal status of AI-generated fan art remains unclear.
  • Late March 2026
    Peak global saturation. Palworld mod replaces a vanilla character with a Gugu Gaga skin. Multiple landing pages compete for the search query.

A starter prompt (image)

The character anchor that keeps results consistent across tools:

image prompt · drinking milk scene
chibi penguin girl in black and white penguin costume, bright yellow beak, big gray eyes, black bangs with silver clips, holding a glass of warm milk with both tiny hands, sitting on a wooden stool, cozy kitchen background, soft warm lighting, cute anime style
motion prompt · for kling / hitpaw / seedance
The chibi penguin girl lifts the glass of milk to her mouth, takes a tiny sip, her eyes close happily, she kicks her feet gently. Warm cozy atmosphere, smooth gentle animation.

Keep the character description identical across all your prompts — that's how you get character consistency without LoRAs. Vary only the scene and the action.

A note on copyright — Gugugaga Penguin is fan-made AI art. The Endministrator character belongs to Hypergryph (developer of Arknights: Endfield). For personal use this is generally treated as fan expression. Commercial use requires more caution given the unresolved copyright dispute.
